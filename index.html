Leona Lassak, Ph.D.

I'm a postdoctoral researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Security and Privacy in Bochum, Germany. My research covers computer security and human-computer interaction (HCI), specializing in passwordless authentication and privacy.

During my Ph.D., I also closely collaborated with legal scholars on data privacy regulations as part of the interdisciplinary research program SecHuman.
